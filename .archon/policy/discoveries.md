# Discoveries policy: Bug Swarm

What agents notice outside the change they were building.

File as a new issue in this repository:

- A bug a player would meet: a broken control, a score or board that is wrong, a layout that breaks at 390 px portrait or in landscape, a frame-rate drop.
- A gap in CI or tests that let, or could let, a broken change through.
- A way a viewer could put an unfiltered name on the stream.

Do not file:

- Style preferences, renames or refactors with no effect a player sees.
- New game features: the owner and the livestream chat choose those.
- Faults in the Archon engine or the factory pack itself: write them in the run's notes; the operator passes them on.
- Anything an open issue already covers.
